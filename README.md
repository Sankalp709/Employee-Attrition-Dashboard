# Employee Attrition Dashboard (Excel)

Interactive Excel dashboard that analyzes employee attrition and shows why employees leave. It helps managers understand attrition and make better decisions.

## What I did
- Used Pivot Tables to summarize 10,000 employee records by department, job level, age group and overtime
- Built charts for employees by department, attrition by department, average salary, performance rating and work-life balance
- Added KPI cards: total employees, average salary, average experience, average performance, attrition rate and promotion rate
- Added slicers (OverTime, Department, JobLevel, AgeGroup) so the dashboard can be filtered easily

## Key insights
- The company has 10,000 employees and the attrition rate is 9.03%
- Sales has the largest workforce (about 2,453 employees) and the most employees leaving (233)
- 45% of employees are rated "Exceeds Expectations"
- Promotion rate is 9.14%

## Tools used
Microsoft Excel (Pivot Tables, Charts, Slicers)
